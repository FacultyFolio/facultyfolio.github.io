Bio

Dr. Craig Gotsman is a Distinguished Professor of Computer Science and the former Dean of the Ying Wu College of Computing (YWCC) at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT), having led the college from 2017 to 2023. Internationally recognized for his pioneering research in 3D computer graphics, geometry processing, and visual computing, he has published over 170 research papers and holds multiple U.S. patents. Dr. Gotsman is a deeply entrepreneurial academic; he was a co-founder of the Cornell Tech campus in New York City and has co-founded three tech startups, including a 3D video processing company that was acquired by Apple. In recognition of his profound impact on both academia and commercial technology, he has been elected as a Fellow of the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM), a Fellow of the National Academy of Inventors (NAI), and a member of the Academy of Europe. He holds a Ph.D., M.S., and B.S. from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.
