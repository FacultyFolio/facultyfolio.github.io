Profile

Bio

Dr. Zhi Wei is a Distinguished Professor at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT), holding a primary appointment in the Department of Computer Science within the Ying Wu College of Computing (YWCC) alongside joint appointments in the Department of Data Science and the Department of Mathematical Sciences. He earned his Ph.D. in Bioinformatics from the University of Pennsylvania (2008), an M.S. in Computer Science from Rutgers University (2004), and a B.S. in Computer Science from Wuhan University (2000). A pioneer in bioinformatics and computational genetics, Dr. Wei’s scholarly research focuses on the intersection of machine learning, statistical learning, and artificial intelligence applied to complex biological data and medical image analysis. His monumental contributions to knowledge discovery from genomic data have earned him international acclaim, including prestigious selections as an IEEE Fellow, an AAAS Fellow (American Association for the Advancement of Science), and an AAIA Fellow. At NJIT, where he also directs the Bioinformatics Program, Dr. Wei bridges foundational algorithms and life sciences by instructing advanced courses in data structures, genomic analysis, and honors machine learning.
