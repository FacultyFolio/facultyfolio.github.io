Bio

Cheickna Sylla is an Associate Professor at the School of Management, New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT), Newark, New Jersey. He holds a Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering from SUNY at Buffalo, New York. His teaching and research interests include decision support modeling and evaluation, statistical analyses, operations management and human factors. His publications appear in IEEE Transactions in Systems, Man and Cybernetics, IEEE Transactions on Engineering Management, European Journal of Operations Research, Decision Sciences, Computers and Industrial Engineering, Computers in Industry, Manufacturing Review, Human Factors, Control Engineering Practice, Cost Management, International Journal of Technology Management, and International Environmental Agreement.
