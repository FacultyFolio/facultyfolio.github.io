Bio

Dr. Taha F. Marhaba is a Distinguished Professor and the Chairman of the John A. Reif, Jr. Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at the Newark College of Engineering, New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT). With extensive expertise in water quality monitoring and environmental infrastructure resilience, his pioneering research includes the development of the Spectral Fluorescent Signatures (SFS) technique to effectively identify hazardous organic matter in drinking water supplies. Alongside his academic leadership, Dr. Marhaba serves as an ABET Accreditation Team Chair, sits on the advisory board of the York Center for Environmental Engineering and Science, and operates as the director of the New Jersey Applied Water Research Center, positioning him as a leading regional expert on drought consequences and modern infrastructure safety.
