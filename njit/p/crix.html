Bio

Dr. Reza Curtmola is a Professor of Computer Science at the Ying Wu College of Computing at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT), where he also serves as the Director of the Computer Science Ph.D. program and Co-director of the NJIT Cybersecurity Research Center. His research focuses on software supply chain security, cloud security, and applied cryptography, highlighted by his co-development of in-toto, a widely used open-source framework for securing software development pipelines. A recipient of the prestigious NSF CAREER award, his work is heavily funded by major agencies like the NSF and DARPA. Dr. Curtmola earned his Ph.D. and M.S. in Computer Science from Johns Hopkins University and continues to actively publish and mentor researchers in the cybersecurity field.
