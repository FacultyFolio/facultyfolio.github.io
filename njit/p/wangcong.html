Bio

Dr. Cong Wang is a faculty member in the ECE department at New Jersey Institute of Technology. Before joining NJIT in 2015, Dr. Wang was a Lecturer and Research Engineer at University of California, Berkeley. He obtained his PhD degree in the area of Controls and Dynamics from UC Berkeley in 2014, before which he attended Tsinghua University and obtained his master's degree in Automotive Engineering and bachelor's degree in Manufacturing Engineering and Automation in 2010 and 2008 respectively.
