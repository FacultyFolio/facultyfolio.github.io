Bio

Dr. Yanxiao Feng joins the School of Applied Engineering and Technology at the New Jersey Institute of Technology as an assistant professor, and she also holds a joint appointment in the Hillier College of Architecture & Design. Her research interests mainly focus on the interface between human factors and the building’s physical environment toward the ultimate goals in terms of building sustainability, resilience, and environmental health.
