Bio

Dr. Haisu Zhang is Martin Tuchman '62 Chair and Associate Professor of Marketing and Entrepreneurship at Martin Tuchman School of Management. His current research interests include product innovation, crowdfunding, technology entrepreneurship, and AI-marketing interface. His research has been published in 20 academic articles, which have been cited by scholars from over 50 countries. Dr. Zhang is an active member of the international academic community. He serves as an associate editor and a member of the editorial review board of several leading academic journals.
