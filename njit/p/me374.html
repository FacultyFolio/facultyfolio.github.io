Bio

I am passionate about teaching and research in the field of Computer Science. My primary teaching interests include Data Structures and Algorithms, and Computational Geometry. My research focuses on Computational Geometry, Algorithms, and Theoretical Computer Science. I am particularly interested in higher education and various pedagogical approaches to enhance student learning. In 2021, I was a research scholar at the University of Memphis. Prior to that, I was an Assistant Professor at Alzahra University in Tehran, Iran from 2009 to 2021.
