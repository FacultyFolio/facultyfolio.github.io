Bio

Dr. David A. Bader is an American computer scientist, Distinguished Professor, and the founder of the Department of Data Science at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT). He also serves as the inaugural Director of the Institute for Data Science at NJIT. Bader is a globally recognized expert in high-performance computing (HPC), data science, and large-scale analytics. The Computer History Museum credits him with developing the first Linux-based supercomputer, an architectural design that became the template for modern supercomputers worldwide.
