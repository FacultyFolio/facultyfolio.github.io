Bio

Dr. Jamie Souvenir is a Professor of Computer Science and the Dean of the Ying Wu College of Computing (YWCC) at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT). She earned her Ph.D. from Washington University in St. Louis and previously served as the chair of the Department of Computer and Information Sciences at Temple University. Dr. Souvenir's primary research centers on pervasive computing systems for smart health, crowdsensing, and evidence-based computer science education. A prominent leader in academic equity, she has secured over $40 million in federal research funding and serves as the director of the STARS Computing Corps, an NSF alliance that connects computing to community impact and successfully broadens STEM participation among women and historically underrepresented groups.
