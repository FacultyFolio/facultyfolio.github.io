Bio

Dr. Donghee Yvette Wohn is an Associate Professor in the Department of Informatics within the Ying Wu College of Computing (YWCC) at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT). She serves as the founder and director of the Social Interaction Lab (formerly Social X Lab), where her research centers on Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and computer-mediated communication. Dr. Wohn specializes in studying the characteristics and behavioral outcomes of social interactions in digital environments, including social media, esports, livestreaming platforms, and the metaverse. Her multi-project research portfolio—supported by the National Science Foundation (NSF), Mozilla Foundation, and Yahoo—investigates social media content moderation, online harassment, digital economies, and creator-supporter dynamics. She is a dedicated mentor, recognized with the NCWIT Mentoring Award for Undergraduate Research. Dr. Wohn holds a Ph.D. in Media and Information Studies from Michigan State University, an M.A. in Journalism from Harvard University, and a B.A. from Ewha Womans University.
