Bio

Dr. Hai Phan (Nhat Hai Phan) is an Associate Professor of Data Science with a joint appointment in the Department of Informatics at the Ying Wu College of Computing (YWCC), within the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT). A founding member and Associate Chair of the Data Science Department, he also serves as the Director of the Data Science Ph.D. program and heads the AI4Good Lab. His research focuses on trustworthy machine learning, data privacy, cybersecurity, and mobile/cloud computing—producing over 90 publications in top-tier venues like USENIX Security, ACM CCS, IEEE S&P, and ICML. Notably, his pioneering work on secure human-AI communications earned a prestigious AAAI Distinguished Paper Award. Backed by the National Science Foundation (NSF) and major industry partners such as Qualcomm, Microsoft, Adobe, and Wells Fargo, Dr. Phan's lab frequently develops innovative, privacy-preserving AI systems. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Montpellier (France), an M.S. from Konkuk University (South Korea), and a B.S. from Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (Vietnam).
