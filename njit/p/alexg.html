Bio

Alexandros Gerbessiotis is an Associate Professor of Computer Science within the Ying Wu College of Computing (YWCC) at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT). His academic work and research primarily focus on high-performance parallel and distributed computing architectures, advanced algorithm design, and the mathematical foundations of computer science. With extensive experience in higher education, he teaches rigorous core courses in data structures, algorithms, and discrete mathematics at both the undergraduate and graduate levels. Beyond teaching, he has contributed to the academic community through peer-reviewed research publications, algorithmic optimizations, and developed extensive educational resources for the computer science department.
