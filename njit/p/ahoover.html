Bio

Dr. Amy K. Hoover is an Assistant Professor of Informatics with a joint appointment in the Department of Computer Science at the Ying Wu College of Computing (YWCC) within the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT). She is the founder and director of the Game AI Methods and Evolutionary Systems (GAIMES) Lab, where her research operates at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and Artificial Intelligence. Dr. Hoover specializes in digital game design, quality diversity optimization, and creative computing—building systems that blend computational power with human artistic judgment to augment creative processes in games, music, and sound. Her outstanding contributions to pedagogy and technological ethics earned her the Matthew J. Hill '99H Honors Faculty Fellowship in Ethics and the Digital Future at NJIT's Albert Dorman Honors College. Dr. Hoover holds a Ph.D. and an M.S. in Computer Science, as well as a B.S. in Computer Science and a B.S. in Pure Mathematics, all from the University of Central Florida.
