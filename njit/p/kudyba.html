Bio

Dr. Kudyba focuses on organizational efficiency and innovation through Analytics (BI and Data Mining), MIS and strategic management. He has published numerous books and articles involving Data Mining and MIS in B2B marketing, Digital Transformation, Data Products, Healthcare, Fintech, Supply Chain and others. He has over 15 years of private sector experience.
