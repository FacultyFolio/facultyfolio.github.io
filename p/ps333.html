Bio

Petras Swissler received his B.S. degree in mechanical engineering from Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology, Terre Haute, IN, USA in 2012 and the Ph.D. degree in mechanical engineering from Northwestern University, Evanston, IL, USA, in 2022.He is an Assistant Professor with the Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering at the New Jersey Institute of Technology, Newark, NJ, USA.
