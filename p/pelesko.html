Bio

Dr. John Pelesko is a distinguished mathematician, academic leader, and the Interim President-elect of the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT), with his presidential tenure officially beginning on August 4, 2026. A proud NJIT alumnus, he earned his Ph.D. in mathematical sciences from the university in the 1990s before building a diverse, 25-year career in higher education as a professor, department chair, and associate dean. Before returning to his alma mater, he spent over two decades at the University of Delaware, where he notably served as the Dean of the College of Arts and Sciences. In 2023, he transitioned to NJIT as the Provost and Executive Vice President for Academic Affairs, overseeing the university's entire academic enterprise, enrollment management, global initiatives, and research portfolio. As a scholar, his research centers on the mathematical modeling of physical, micro, and nanoscale engineered systems. He has published highly regarded textbooks on self-assembly and microelectromechanical systems (MEMS), and he continues to champion student success by spearheading cutting-edge academic frameworks like the AI@NJIT initiative.
