Bio

Professor Cummings studied for her BA and PhD at the University of Oxford. She held postdoctoral positions at U. Oxford, Technion IIT, Ecole Normale Superieure (Paris), and a faculty position at the University of Nottingham, before coming to NJIT in 2008.
