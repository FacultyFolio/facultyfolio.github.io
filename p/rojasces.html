Bio

Roberto Rojas-Cessa obtained a MS Degree in Electrical Engineering from CINVESTAV (MX), and a MS degree in Computer Engineering and PhD degree in Electrical Engineering from Polytechnic University (now Tandon School of Engineering NYU). He is active in research on networking, data analysis, and energy. He is interesting in developing new concepts and in making them a reality, through translational research. He has published extensively on various specialized journals and conference proceedings, and presented his work on various International conferences. He co-authored the books “Advanced Internet Protocols, Services, and Applications," Wiley and Sons, 2012 and “Interconnections for Computer Communications and Packet Networks," CRC Press, 2017. His research has been funded by U.S. National Science Foundation and private companies. He is a recipient of the New Jersey Inventors Hall of Fame Innovator Award in 2013, and the NJIT NCE Excellent Teacher Award 2013. He serves in different capacities for IEEE conferences and journals, and as an officer of the Communications Switching and Routing Technical Committee for several years.
