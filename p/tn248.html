Bio

Thi Phong Nguyen studied for her PhD in Applied Mathematics at CMAP (Centre de Mathématiques Appliquées) at École Polytechnique, France. She held Postdoctoral Associate positions at Rutgers University, New Brunwick and University of Minnesota, Twins-cities, and Golomb Visiting Assistant Professor position at Purdue University, West Lafayette, before joining NJIT in 2022.
