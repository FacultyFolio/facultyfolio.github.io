Bio

Dr. Fadi P. Deek is a Distinguished Professor of Informatics and Mathematical Sciences and the former Provost and Senior Executive Vice President at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT), spanning over four decades of academic leadership and service to the institution. He holds unique dual faculty appointments within the Department of Informatics (Ying Wu College of Computing) and the Department of Mathematical Sciences (College of Science and Liberal Arts), alongside serving as NJIT’s Then and Clark Endowed Chair. His extensive research focuses on learning systems, computing education, and collaborative software development architectures, complemented by a deeply personal literary contribution in his recent memoir, Portrait of an Expatriate: A Lebanese-American Story, which chronicles his journey from a war-torn Lebanon to becoming a cornerstone academic leader in America. A true lifelong Highlander, Dr. Deek earned his B.S. and M.S. in Computer Science, as well as his Ph.D. in Computer and Information Science, all as an alumnus of NJIT.
