Bio

Dr. Samuel C. Lieber, P.E. is an Associate Professor, Director, and Department Chair of the School of Applied Engineering and Technology (SAET) within the Newark College of Engineering at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT), where he also holds a joint appointment in the Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering. A three-time alumnus of NJIT, he holds a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering and is a registered Professional Engineer. Dr. Lieber's interdisciplinary research spans advanced manufacturing, mechatronics education, numerical modeling, and medical device optimization, with specific expertise in simulating finishing processes for additively manufactured metal components. In his leadership role at SAET, he focuses on bridging the industrial skills gap, advancing laboratory instruction, and aligning educational curricula with regional workforce needs and professional licensure pipelines.
