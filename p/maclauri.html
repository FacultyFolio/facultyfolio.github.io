Bio

I was born in Australia, and studied Pure Mathematics and Philosophy for my undergraduate degree at the University of New South Wales. I completed a Phd at Oxford in 2014, under the supervision of Prof. Jon Chapman and Tiina Roose. Postdoctoral experience at INRIA Sophia Antipolis, France, University of Sydney and University of Utah
