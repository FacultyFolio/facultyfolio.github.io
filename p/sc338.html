Bio

Sara C. Zapico is an assistant professor at the Department of Chemistry and Environmental Science in the College of Science and Liberal Arts. She is also a Research Collaborator at Smithsonian Institution. Her laboratory, "Interdisciplinary Forensics and Biomedical Sciences Lab" (ForenBioS) focuses on the application of biochemical techniques to forensic sciences issues like age-at-death estimation, post-mortem interval determination, body fluid identification, and DNA extraction from tough substrates, with implications in biomedical sciences and cell biology.
