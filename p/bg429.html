Bio

I am an Assistant Professor of Chemical and Materials Engineering at NJIT and the Principal Investigator of the RAMP Lab (Reactive & Adaptive Membrane Processes Lab). My research focuses on next-generation membrane materials and separation processes for water treatment, CO₂ capture, gas purification, hydrogen recovery, and biopharmaceutical processing. At RAMP, we engineer intelligent membranes that not only separate but also react — integrating nanozymes, photocatalysts, electrocatalysts, and porous materials into polymer, inorganic, and hybrid platforms. Our goal is to tackle global challenges in environment, energy, and life sciences by shaping the future of separation and conversion. Before joining NJIT in Fall 2025, I served as Principal Scientist and R&D Manager at Vandstrom Inc. (2023–2025), where I led membrane innovation projects bridging fundamental research and industrial application. Prior to that, I conducted postdoctoral research at iCeMS, Kyoto University (2013–2016), and later served as Assistant and Associate Professor at Kyoto University (2016–2023). I earned my Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering from Tarbiat Modares University (2013). To date, I have published 50+ peer-reviewed papers, contributed to patents and book chapters, and served on editorial boards of journals in membrane, polymer, and materials science.
