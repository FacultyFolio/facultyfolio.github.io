Bio

Dr. Janice Daniel is a Professor in the Civil and Environmental Engineering Department. She has been a Professor at NJIT since 1999 teaching courses in Traffic Control and Capacity Analysis and performing research in the areas of Traffic Operations and Transportation Safety. Dr. Daniel began her career working in the Traffic Engineering Division of the Port Authority of NY and NJ. She has also worked as a Transportation Engineer for a transportation consulting firm. She has served as the Associate Chair for Graduate Studies in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering. She has also served as the Associate Dean for Research and Graduate Studies in the Newark College of Engineering.
