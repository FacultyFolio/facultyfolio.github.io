Bio

Bruce Bukiet is an applied mathematician who has made contributions to numerous areas of mathematical modeling, including detonations, insect infestation,applications of math to biology and baseball. He also works on promoting STEM for K-career to increase the number of people, especially among underrepresented groups, who pursue STEM learning. Related interests include online professional development and digital learning.
