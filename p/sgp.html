Bio

Stephen Pemberton, Ph.D. , is Chair of the NJIT Federated Department of History Dr. Pemberton is a historian of medicine, disease, and health with expertise in United States history and the history and sociology of science. His research and teaching is also informed by his training in philosophy and his engagements with medical humanities scholarship and health policy debates. He is author of The Bleeding Disease: Hemophilia and the Unintended Consequences of Medical Progress (Johns Hopkins University Press, 2011) and co-author of The Troubled Dream of Genetic Medicine: Ethnicity and Innovation in Tay-Sachs, Cystic Fibrosis and Sickle Cell Disease (Johns Hopkins University Press, 2006). His current research project is ‘Bleeding Disorder: What Hemophilia Experiences Reveal about Biocapitalism and Health Advocacy.’
