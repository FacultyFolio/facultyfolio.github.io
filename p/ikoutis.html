Bio

Dr. Ioannis Koutis is a Professor of Computer Science and the Associate Chair of Graduate Studies at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT). After earning his Ph.D. from Carnegie Mellon University, he pioneered breakthrough algorithms in spectral graph theory, notably developing some of the world's fastest solvers for Symmetric Diagonally Dominant (SDD) linear systems alongside collaborators Gary Miller and Richard Peng. His research bridges fundamental mathematical theory with high-impact applications, including algebraic fingerprints for hard computational problems, massive speedups in electronic circuit design (EDA), and advanced machine learning models applied to computational biology.
