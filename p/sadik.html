Bio

Dr. Sadik is Vice Provost for Faculty Affairs and a Distinguished Professor of Chemistry and Environmental Science. She is also the Director of the NJIT Bio S ensors M aterials for A dvanced R esearch & T echnology (The BioSMART Center). She received her Ph.D. in Chemistry from the University of Wollongong in Wollongong, Australia, and was a National Research Council postdoctoral fellow at the United States Environmental Protection Agency (EPA). She has held appointments at the State University of New York at Binghamton, Harvard University, Cornell University, and the Naval Research Laboratory. Dr. Sadik is a Naturalized US Citizen.
