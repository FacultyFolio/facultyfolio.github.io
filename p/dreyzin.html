Bio

Prof. Dreizin received his MS and PhD degrees from Odessa University, Ukraine in 1985 and 1992, respectively. In the US, he worked as a research scientist at AeroChem Research Labs at Princeton, NJ from 1993 to 1999. He joined NJIT faculty in 1999.
