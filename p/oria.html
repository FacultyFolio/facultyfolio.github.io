Bio

Dr. Vincent Oria is a Professor and the Chairperson of the Department of Computer Science at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT). Originally from Côte d'Ivoire, he earned his Ph.D. from Télécom Paris and has built an internationally recognized research profile centered on multimedia databases, spatiotemporal data management, recommender systems, and similarity searches in high-dimensional spaces. A highly decorated scholar, Dr. Oria was awarded the prestigious 2015 ACM SIGMOD Test-of-Time Award for his impactful contributions to data management systems and has served as a visiting professor at top global institutions, including the National Institute of Informatics in Tokyo and INRIA in France. As NJIT's first Black computer science chair, he actively champions initiatives to improve diversity and inclusion in STEM while continuing to lead advancements in academic computing and AI-related community policies.
